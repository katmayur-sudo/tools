# Anxiety reel: HeyGen + SubMagic workflow

**The idea:** your voice plays from start to end. Your avatar shows only in the face shots: the hook (1), the intro (2) and the ending (11). For everything in between (shots 3–10), the screen shows **only the card**: no avatar, no face, no logo. Your voice keeps playing underneath.

| Part | What is on screen | What is heard |
|---|---|---|
| Shots 1, 2, 11 | HeyGen avatar (full screen) + captions | Your voice |
| Shots 3, 5–10 | Card full screen + captions | Your voice |
| Shot 4 | "3 TYPES" word card, **no captions** | Your voice |

---

## Step 1: Record the voice note
- Read `01_Script_to_read.md` exactly as written (Roman Hinglish, 11 numbered lines).
- Quiet room: fan and AC off, door closed. Soft furnishings (bed, curtains) cut echo.
- Phone 15–20 cm from your mouth, slightly to the side so breath doesn't hit the mic. Same distance the whole time.
- One continuous take. **Stay silent for 1 second between numbered lines.** These gaps are where the cards switch.
- If you fumble, pause, then say the **whole line** again. The last clean take is kept and the rest is cut.
- Calm, warm, a bit slower than normal. Smile on the hook and the ending.
- Save as the highest-quality voice recording your phone allows (WAV or M4A). Don't use WhatsApp, which compresses audio.

## Step 2: Send the voice note to me (recommended)
I'll return, within the same chat:
1. **Clean audio:** background noise removed, retakes/false starts/long pauses cut, levelled to the Instagram loudness. Use **this** file in HeyGen.
2. **Exact cue sheet:** every card's in and out time in seconds and frames, so nothing is placed by eye.
3. **Captions (.srt)** in Roman Hinglish, timed word by word to your voice, to check SubMagic's captions against.
4. Optional: **one video clip per card shot** with the pop-ins already animated, each exactly as long as its slot. In SubMagic you then drop 8 clips instead of 33 images.

(If you skip this step, the cue sheet below works by spoken words instead of times.)

## Step 3: HeyGen (avatar video)
- New video → **9:16 portrait (1080 x 1920)** → choose your avatar.
- Script: **upload audio** and use the clean audio from step 2 (or your voice note).
- Background: plain and calm (solid dark navy or off-white), so the cuts between avatar and cards feel smooth.
- No HeyGen captions, no text, no logo (SubMagic does captions; the cards carry the graphics).
- Generate and export **1080p MP4**. The avatar talks the whole time; during card shots it will be covered.

## Step 4: SubMagic (captions + cards)
1. **Upload** the HeyGen video. Language: Hindi/Hinglish.
2. **Captions:**
   - Choose Roman (Latin) script if offered, then proofread every line against `01_Script_to_read.md`. Medical words often come out wrong: anhedonia, agoraphobia, hyperventilation, phaeochromocytoma, benzodiazepine.
   - Position: lower-middle, about 65–75% down the screen. All cards keep that band empty, so captions never cover a card.
   - **Delete the captions during shot 4** (the "3 TYPES / PHOBIC PANIC GENERALISED" card), which is meant to be words only.
3. **Cards (B-roll / your own media):**
   - Upload the images from `02_Cards_step_by_step/`, or from `03_Cards_one_per_shot/` if you want no pop-ins.
   - Put each one in at its cue word (table below), and keep it until the next image or the next face shot.
   - Set every card to **full screen / cover**, not split-screen or picture-in-picture. **The avatar must not be visible at all while a card is on.**
   - Keep the **original audio** of the video under the cards (don't mute it), so your voice continues.
   - The cards are opaque, full-frame 1080x1920 images, so they hide the avatar completely when set to full screen.
4. **Hook text (shot 1, optional):** add a text "Heart attack ya Panic attack?" over the avatar in the first 3 seconds.
5. **Music:** soft, calm, low (well under the voice). **Sound effects:** optional soft whoosh at each card change.
6. **Export:** 1080 x 1920, 30 fps, highest quality.

## Step 5: Before posting (checklist)
- [ ] No avatar, face or logo visible during any card (shots 3–10). Scrub through slowly.
- [ ] Every card appears on its cue word and stays until the next one.
- [ ] Captions match what you said, word for word, and are absent on the "3 TYPES" card.
- [ ] Voice is clear all the way through; music never covers words.
- [ ] Ending shows the avatar again from "OCD aur PTSD agle reel mein".
- [ ] Cover and Instagram caption (ask me to make them, as for the mood reel).
- [ ] Photo credit line in the caption (`04_Photo_credits.md`).
