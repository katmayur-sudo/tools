## How to record (lessons from the last two takes)
- **Read the whole line first, then look up and say it.** Every glance down while speaking has to be hidden or cut, and a few words were lost to cards last time.
- **Hold the mic below your chin, not in front of your mouth.** Lips stay visible, which looks better and makes perfect lip sync easier.
- 1 second of silence between numbered parts. If you fumble, pause and repeat the **whole** sentence; I keep the last clean take.
- Phone vertical, face in the upper half, plain wall, light from the front, quiet room.
- Send the raw file as it is.

## What I do after
Cut to the script (clean takes, no pauses, breaths or glances down), sync, clean voice, cards, captions, music and SFX, then either a finished reel or a DaVinci Resolve kit, whichever you want.
