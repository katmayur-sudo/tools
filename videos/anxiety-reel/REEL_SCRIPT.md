### Fact check (every on-screen fact)
| Line | Davidson 24th ed |
|---|---|
| 4 features: anxiety, worrisome thoughts, avoidance, autonomic symptoms | p1254 |
| Somatic symptoms make patients go to medical services first | p1254 |
| 3 types: phobic, panic, generalised | p1254, Box 31.29 |
| Phobia = excessive fear of an object/situation + avoidance; flying; agoraphobia (crowds / going out alone); venepuncture | p1255 |
| Panic: repeated severe attacks; chest pain, palpitations, paraesthesia in lips and fingers; fear of heart attack or stroke; due partly to hyperventilation | p1255 |
| Panic confirmed by deliberate hyperventilation for 1–2 min; respiratory alkalosis = chronic hyperventilation | p1255 |
| GAD: chronic uncontrollable worry; muscle tension → medical presentations | p1255 |
| Organic causes: hyperthyroidism, paroxysmal arrhythmias, phaeochromocytoma, hypoglycaemia, alcohol and benzodiazepine withdrawal (TLE left off-screen for time) | p1242, Box 31.11 |
| Explanation and reassurance essential; relaxation, graded exposure, CBT; antidepressants first choice; benzodiazepines short term only (dependence) | p1255 |
