Psychiatry has hundreds of diagnoses. ICD-10 fits them all into just 10 blocks 🧠

Here they are, straight from Davidson:

1️⃣ F00–F09 Organic: dementia, delirium
2️⃣ F10–F19 Psychoactive substances: intoxication → dependence → withdrawal
3️⃣ F20–F29 Schizophrenia & delusional disorders
4️⃣ F30–F39 Mood: depression, bipolar disorder
5️⃣ F40–F48 Neurotic, stress-related & somatoform: phobias, GAD, OCD, PTSD
6️⃣ F50–F59 Physiological disturbance: anorexia, bulimia, sexual dysfunction
7️⃣ F60–F69 Adult personality & behaviour
8️⃣ F70–F79 Mental retardation (now: intellectual disability)
9️⃣ F80–F89 Psychological development: autism, Asperger syndrome
🔟 F90–F98 Childhood: hyperkinetic & tic disorders

🩺 Ward pearl: on medical wards, delirium is very common. Always think organic first.

Note: this follows the ICD-10 table in Davidson. ICD-11 (in use since 2022) regroups some of these.

Source: Davidson's Principles & Practice of Medicine, Medical Psychiatry (WHO ICD-10). For education only, not medical advice.
