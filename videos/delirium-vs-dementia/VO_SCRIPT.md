# Voice-over script: Delirium vs Dementia (Hinglish)

Source: Davidson's Principles & Practice of Medicine, 24th ed (2022). Not from the book: the celebrity
diagnoses (public reports), the "Dadaji... budhapa hai" line and the closing "Save karo" line.

About 355 words, about 2 min 20 s. Read the words exactly as written (captions use them), one sentence per
line; speed is up to you, the video re-times itself to your recording. One take, WAV or MP3, quiet room, no music.

**Hook**  
Robin Williams, Bruce Willis, Ronald Reagan, aur apne George Fernandes.  
Sabko dementia hua.  
Par jab Dadaji achanak confuse ho jaayein, aur ghar wale bolein budhapa hai, to galat!  
Ye delirium ho sakta hai.  
Aaj samjho dono ka fark, sabse simple tarike se.  

**Kaun sa block**  
Dono psychiatric disorders hain, ICD-10 ke pehle block mein: organic mental disorders.  

**Definition**  
Delirium matlab achanak aayi confusion, kisi bimari ya dawa se.  
Dementia matlab dheere dheere yaaddasht aur soch ka jaana, itna ki roz ke kaam bigdein.  

**Fark 1**  
Pehla fark, shuruaat.  
Delirium achanak, ghanton ya dinon mein.  
Dementia dheere, mahinon ya saalon mein.  

**Fark 2**  
Doosra, course.  
Delirium upar neeche hota rehta hai.  
Dementia lagataar badhta hai.  

**Fark 3**  
Teesra, pehli problem.  
Delirium mein dhyan nahi tikta.  
Dementia mein pehle yaaddasht jaati hai.  

**Fark 4**  
Chautha, hosh.  
Delirium mein hosh dhundhla.  
Dementia mein patient alert.  

**Fark 5**  
Paanchwa, hallucinations.  
Delirium mein aksar cheezein dikhti hain.  
Dementia mein dikhein, to Lewy body socho, jaise Robin Williams.  

**Fark 6**  
Chhatha, wajah.  
Delirium ke peeche acute bimari ya dawa, jaise infection ya alcohol withdrawal.  
Dementia ke peeche brain ki dheemi bimari, jaise Alzheimer's.  

**Fark 7**  
Saatwa, kya theek hoga?  
Delirium aksar theek ho jata hai.  
Dementia mostly nahi.  

**Fark 8**  
Aathwa, test.  
Delirium ke liye 4AT.  
Dementia ke liye MoCA.  
Aur haan, dono saath bhi ho sakte hain.  

**Myth 1**  
Ab myths.  
Myth one: budhape mein confusion normal hai.  
Nahi!  
Hospital mein har chautha patient delirium se guzarta hai.  

**Myth 2**  
Myth two: delirium matlab shor machana.  
Nahi!  
Chupchaap hypoactive delirium sabse zyada miss hota hai.  

**Myth 3**  
Myth three: bhoolna matlab dementia.  
Nahi!  
Roz ke kaam na bigdein, to dementia nahi.  
Depression bhi dementia jaisa dikh sakta hai.  

**Myth 4**  
Myth four: delirium theek, kahani khatam.  
Nahi!  
Delirium aage dementia ka bada risk factor hai.  

**Management (Delirium)**  
Ab management.  
Delirium mein cause dhoondo aur theek karo, dawayein check karo, ghadi, chashma aur family se orient karo.  
Paani, khana, neend ka dhyan.  
Sedation pehla ilaaj nahi.  

**Management (Dementia)**  
Dementia mein pehle B12, thyroid aur depression jaise theek hone wale causes dekho.  
Patient aur family ko support do.  
Alzheimer's mein dawa progression thoda slow karti hai.  

**Exam ke liye**  
Exam ke liye ek line.  
Delirium: achanak, fluctuating, attention gaya.  
Dementia: dheere, progressive, patient alert.  

**Close**  
Save karo, aur us dost ko bhejo jo dono ko same samajhta hai.
