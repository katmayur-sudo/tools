# Voice-over script: Delirium vs Dementia (Hinglish)

Source: Davidson's Principles & Practice of Medicine, 24th ed (2022). Every line is from the book except the
"Dadaji... budhapa hai. Galat!" hook and the closing "Save karo" line.

About 335 words, about 2 min 15 s at a steady pace. Read the words exactly as written (captions use them);
speed is up to you, the video re-times itself to your recording. One take, WAV or MP3, quiet room, no music.

**Hook**  
Dadaji achanak confuse ho gaye, ghar wale bole, budhapa hai. Galat! Ye delirium ho sakta hai. Aaj samjho dono ka fark, sabse simple tarike se.

**Kaun sa block**  
Dono psychiatric disorders hain, ICD-10 ke pehle block mein: organic mental disorders.

**Definition**  
Delirium matlab achanak aayi confusion, kisi bimari ya dawa se. Dementia matlab dheere dheere yaaddasht aur soch ka jaana, itna ki roz ke kaam bigdein.

**Fark 1**  
Pehla fark, shuruaat. Delirium achanak, ghanton ya dinon mein. Dementia dheere, mahinon ya saalon mein.

**Fark 2**  
Doosra, course. Delirium upar neeche hota rehta hai. Dementia lagataar badhta hai.

**Fark 3**  
Teesra, pehli problem. Delirium mein dhyan nahi tikta. Dementia mein pehle yaaddasht jaati hai.

**Fark 4**  
Chautha, hosh. Delirium mein hosh dhundhla. Dementia mein patient alert.

**Fark 5**  
Paanchwa, hallucinations. Delirium mein aksar cheezein dikhti hain. Dementia mein dikhein, to Lewy body socho.

**Fark 6**  
Chhatha, wajah. Delirium ke peeche acute bimari ya dawa, jaise infection ya alcohol withdrawal. Dementia ke peeche brain ki dheemi bimari, jaise Alzheimer's.

**Fark 7**  
Saatwa, kya theek hoga? Delirium aksar theek ho jata hai. Dementia mostly nahi.

**Fark 8**  
Aathwa, test. Delirium ke liye 4AT. Dementia ke liye MoCA. Aur haan, dono saath bhi ho sakte hain.

**Myth 1**  
Ab myths. Myth one: budhape mein confusion normal hai. Nahi! Hospital mein har chautha patient delirium se guzarta hai.

**Myth 2**  
Myth two: delirium matlab shor machana. Nahi! Chupchaap hypoactive delirium sabse zyada miss hota hai.

**Myth 3**  
Myth three: bhoolna matlab dementia. Nahi! Roz ke kaam na bigdein, to dementia nahi. Depression bhi dementia jaisa dikh sakta hai.

**Myth 4**  
Myth four: delirium theek, kahani khatam. Nahi! Delirium aage dementia ka bada risk factor hai.

**Management (Delirium)**  
Ab management. Delirium mein cause dhoondo aur theek karo, dawayein check karo, ghadi, chashma aur family se orient karo. Paani, khana, neend ka dhyan. Sedation pehla ilaaj nahi.

**Management (Dementia)**  
Dementia mein pehle B12, thyroid aur depression jaise theek hone wale causes dekho. Patient aur family ko support do. Alzheimer's mein dawa progression thoda slow karti hai.

**Exam ke liye**  
Exam ke liye ek line. Delirium: achanak, fluctuating, attention gaya. Dementia: dheere, progressive, patient alert.

**Close**  
Save karo, aur us dost ko bhejo jo dono ko same samajhta hai.
