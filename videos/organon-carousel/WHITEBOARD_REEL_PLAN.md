# Whiteboard reels: Homoeopathic case-taking (Organon §83–90)

**Idea:** you at a whiteboard, building a real-looking case sheet live while you explain each aphorism. After each step the aphorism card (§83–90) flashes full screen for 3–5 s while your voice continues, then the video cuts back to you at the board.

Content rule: every teaching point comes from §83–90 (Organon, 6th ed). The hooks and the mock patient are teaching devices, not from the book. The mock case is only an illustration of *how to ask*, not a treatment.

---

## 1. How to record the A-roll

**Setup**
- Phone **vertical (9:16)** on a tripod at chest height, about 1.5–2 m from the board. The board fills the frame, with you standing to the **left** of it so you don't block the writing.
- Light from the front or side, falling on both you and the board. Avoid glare: tilt the board or move the light until there is no white shine.
- Mic: a clip-on mic or phone close by. Board marker squeak is loud, so pause speaking while you write long words.
- Plain wall, white coat or solid colour.

**Board layout (draw the empty template before recording)**

```
 ┌──────────────────────── CASE SHEET ────────────────────────┐
 │ PATIENT SAYS (own words)      │ COMPLETE IT (§86)          │
 │ 1. ......................     │ Kab? Kya? Kahan? Kitni der? │
 │ 2. ......................     │ Kab badhta/ghatta? Position │
 │ 3. ......................     │                             │
 ├───────────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────┤
 │ GAPS (§88): Neend · Bhookh · Pyaas · Stool · Urine · Mann   │
 ├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
 │ PHYSICIAN SEES (§90): ..........................            │
 └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Marker colours** (say this once in the video so viewers learn the code)
- **Black:** patient's own words.
- **Blue:** your questions.
- **Red:** don'ts (leading questions).
- **Green:** what you observe.

**Recording tips**
- Record **section by section**, in order (board writing can't be undone mid-take). Before each section, take one clean photo of the board, so I can use it as a still if a take fails.
- Look at the **camera** when you speak, and at the **board** only while you write. Don't speak while writing; I'll cut the writing pauses short.
- Write **big**: about 3 lines per board quarter. Phone viewers must read it.
- Leave 1 second of silence after each aphorism point. That's where the card goes in.
- If you fumble, pause and repeat the whole sentence. I keep the last clean take.

**Edit pattern (every section)**

`A-ROLL (you explain + write) → CARD §xx (3–5 s, your voice continues) → A-ROLL (next step)`

- Cards in the video need the **9:16** version (1080x1920). I'll make those from the same 8 cards.
- Optional extra: punch-in zoom on the board when you point at a line.

## 6. Long version for YouTube (6–8 min, horizontal or vertical)

1. **Hook** (Script A line 1), then what viewers will learn.
2. **§83:** the physician's 4 qualities (1 min).
3. **§84–85:** listening and writing, done live with the mock patient (1.5 min).
4. **§86:** complete 2 symptoms fully on the board (1.5 min).
5. **§87:** right vs wrong questions, 3 pairs in red and blue (1 min).
6. **§88–89:** general first, then special questions, with examples from the footnotes (1.5 min).
7. **§90:** what to observe, using a checklist on the board (1 min).
8. **Recap:** the full board, plus "save / subscribe".

Cards appear after each section (same as the reels).
