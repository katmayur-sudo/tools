# Whiteboard reel: simple point-to-point script (Organon §83–90)

**Before recording:** draw this empty box on the board 👇

```
PATIENT SAYS          | COMPLETE IT
1.                    |
2.                    |
GAPS: Neend · Bhookh · Pyaas · Stool · Urine · Mann
DOCTOR SEES:
```

Markers: **black** = patient · **blue** = your question · **red** = wrong · **green** = observation.
After every step: **stop 1 second** (the card goes there).

---

## Script A: "Ek patient aaya" (~80 s)

**STEP 1 · HOOK** (look at camera)
- Say: *"Homoeopath sirf 'kaisa lag raha hai' nahi poochta. Dekho asli case-taking."*
- Write: nothing.

**STEP 2 · §83**
- Say: *"Doctor mein 4 cheezein: no prejudice, sharp senses, attention, sach likhna."*
- Write (top corner): **No prejudice · Senses · Attention · Fidelity**
- Card: **§83**

**STEP 3 · §84**
- Say: *"Patient bole: 'Sar dard rehta hai.' Main chup. Roko mat. Uske words mein likho."*
- Write (black): **1. "Sar dard rehta hai"**
- Card: **§84**

**STEP 4 · §85**
- Say: *"Har naya symptom, nayi line."*
- Write (black): **2. ……** (leave the line empty)
- Card: **§85**

**STEP 5 · §86**
- Say: *"Ab complete karo: kab se? kaisa? kahan? kitni der? kab badhta?"*
- Write (blue, next to line 1): **Kab · Kaisa · Kahan · Kitni der · Kab badhta**
- Card: **§86**

**STEP 6 · §87**
- Say: *"Galat: 'Dhoop mein badhta hai?' Sahi: 'Kis cheez se badhta hai?'"*
- Write: red ❌ **Dhoop mein badhta?** · blue ✅ **Kis se badhta?**
- Card: **§87**

**STEP 7 · §88**
- Say: *"Jo nahi bataya, general sawaal se poochho: 'Neend kaisi hai?'"*
- Write: tick ✓ the GAPS row
- Card: **§88**

**STEP 8 · §89**
- Say: *"Picture poori ho, tab special sawaal: 'Neend mein bolte ho?'"*
- Write (blue): **Special Q → baad mein**
- Card: **§89**

**STEP 9 · §90**
- Say: *"Aakhir mein doctor khud dekhe: chehra, mood, zubaan, pulse."*
- Write (green, DOCTOR SEES row): **Chehra · Mood · Zubaan · Pulse**
- Card: **§90**

**STEP 10 · END** (step back, show the full board)
- Say: *"Patient ki story plus doctor ka observation, yahi case-taking. Save karo."*

## Script B: "Ek galat sawaal" (~45 s)

1. **Hook:** *"Ek galat sawaal poora case bigaad deta hai."* Write red ❌ **Dhoop mein badhta?**
2. *"Iska jawaab sirf haan ya na. Jawaab sawaal mein hi hai."* Underline **haan/na**.
3. *"Patient aalas mein ya aapko khush karne ke liye haan bol dega."* Write **galat picture → galat dawa**. Card: **§87**
4. *"Sahi: 'Kis cheez se badhta hai?' Phir chup raho."* Write blue ✅ **Kis se badhta?** Card: **§84**
5. **End:** *"Ye galti mat karna. Batchmate ko share karo."*
