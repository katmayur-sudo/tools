# BHMS Psychology reel series: topic list from the syllabus

Source: NCH *Competency Based Dynamic Curriculum for First BHMS* (2022–23 onwards), Psychology section,
PDF pages 57–140 (owner shares it as a Google Drive PDF; ask for the link, don't commit the PDF).
Page numbers below are **PDF page numbers** (printed numbers are about one lower).
This file is only the syllabus map: it says *what* to teach, not the facts. Facts for each reel come from
a textbook the owner picks (the syllabus lists Morgan, NCERT XI/XII, Baron & Misra, Atkinson & Hilgard… p140).

Tags: **M** = must know, **D** = desirable to know, **N** = nice to know (syllabus labels; exam questions follow them).
One line = one reel.

## Exam pattern (Paper HomUG-OM-I, Section II Psychology: 50 marks; p131–137)
- Q1 MCQ 5 × 1 mark (themes B + C) · Q2 SAQ 5 × 5 marks (all themes) · Q3 LAQ 2 × 10 marks (themes B + C only).
- Practical: viva 20 + internal assessment 5.
- Theme weightage: **A** Introduction 5 marks (SAQ only) · **B** Psychological organisation of mind, structural
  and functional, 25 marks (MCQ/SAQ/LAQ) · **C** Growth and development 10 marks (MCQ/SAQ/LAQ) ·
  **D** Personality and stress 5 marks (SAQ only) · **E** Applied psychology 5 marks (SAQ only).
- So themes B and C carry 35 of 50 marks and are the only LAQ source: make those reels first.
- Theme mapping below is inferred from topic titles (the syllabus does not map topics to themes one by one).
