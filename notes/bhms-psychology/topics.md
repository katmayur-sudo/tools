# BHMS Psychology reel series: topic list from the syllabus, mapped to the textbook

**Topics** come from the NCH *Competency Based Dynamic Curriculum for First BHMS* (2022–23 onwards), Psychology
section, syllabus PDF pages 57–140 (`syl p…`).

**Facts** come only from the owner's psychology textbook (owner's rule, 2026-11-01): *Introduction to Psychology*,
Saylor Foundation edition, 783 pages, CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 (`book p…`, = PDF page = printed page). The owner shares both
as Google Drive PDFs: ask for the links, download to the scratchpad, never commit them. The owner first thought the
book was Morgan's; it is not, so never cite it as Morgan. Explain in your own words; don't copy text or figures
(the licence is non-commercial).

Coverage: no mark = the book covers it · **◐** = only partly, say what's missing · **✗** = not in the book. For ◐/✗,
mark anything taken from elsewhere as outside the book (Repertory/Materia Medica parts need the owner's homoeopathy
books).

Tags: **M** = must know, **D** = desirable to know, **N** = nice to know (syllabus labels; exam questions follow
them). One line = one reel.

## Exam pattern (Paper HomUG-OM-I, Section II Psychology: 50 marks; syl p131–137)
- Q1 MCQ 5 × 1 mark (themes B + C) · Q2 SAQ 5 × 5 marks (all themes) · Q3 LAQ 2 × 10 marks (themes B + C only).
- Practical: viva 20 + internal assessment 5.
- Theme weightage: **A** Introduction 5 marks (SAQ only) · **B** Psychological organisation of mind, structural
  and functional, 25 marks (MCQ/SAQ/LAQ) · **C** Growth and development 10 marks (MCQ/SAQ/LAQ) ·
  **D** Personality and stress 5 marks (SAQ only) · **E** Applied psychology 5 marks (SAQ only).
- So themes B and C carry 35 of 50 marks and are the only LAQ source: make those reels first.
- Theme mapping below is inferred from topic titles (the syllabus does not map topics to themes one by one).
